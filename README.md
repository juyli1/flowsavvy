## What Actually Is FlowSavvy?

FlowSavvy is this AI-powered scheduling assistant that automatically time-blocks your to-do list into your calendar. You tell it what needs to get done and when it's due, and it figures out when you should actually do it. The clever bit? When your plans inevitably change (because whose plans don't?), it automatically reshuffles everything for you.

Think of it like having a really organized friend who's always looking at your calendar and saying, "Hey, you've got three hours free on Tuesday afternoon—perfect time to knock out that project you've been avoiding."

## How It Actually Works

Here's the thing that makes FlowSavvy different from your standard calendar app: it's proactive instead of reactive.

Most calendar apps are basically just digital versions of paper calendars. You put stuff in, it sits there, and you're responsible for figuring out when you'll actually do everything. FlowSavvy flips this around.

You create a task, set when it's due, estimate how long it'll take, and FlowSavvy automatically finds the best time to schedule it based on your available hours. Got a meeting that runs long? The whole schedule adjusts. Finish something early? Everything else shifts up.

The color-coding system is surprisingly helpful too. Tasks turn from green to orange to red as their deadlines approach, so you can see at a glance what's getting urgent.

## The Features That Actually Matter

**Auto-Splitting**: This one's clever. If you have a three-hour task but only one-hour gaps in your schedule, FlowSavvy will split it across multiple time slots. No more "I don't have enough time to start this" excuses.

**Buffer Time**: You can set automatic breaks between tasks. Because let's be real—jumping straight from a two-hour meeting into deep work doesn't actually work.

**Workload Balancing**: FlowSavvy can either front-load your tasks (get them done ASAP) or spread them out evenly across days. Depends on how you like to work.

**The Recalculate Button**: This is my favorite feature. When you inevitably fall behind schedule, just hit recalculate and FlowSavvy reorganizes everything that's left. It's like having a reset button for your week.

## Where It Falls Short

👉 [No official API yet](https://flowsavvy.app), though some users have built workarounds. If you need deep integrations with other tools, that might be limiting.
